# Exercise: Predicting the Market for Tesla Stock

## Overview

This project focused on developing a functional Jupyter Notebook capable of analyzing and predicting movements in the Tesla stock market using machine learning models. The main idea was to explore whether patterns in historical data and engineered financial indicators could help anticipate whether the stock price is likely to rise or not. 

The notebook was built step-by-step to reflect both trading and long-term investment perspectives, and aimed to provide predictions that were statistically evaluated rather than purely speculative. Special care was taken to integrate various supervised learning models, adjust their parameters, and interpret the output in a meaningful way based on real metrics like accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score.
